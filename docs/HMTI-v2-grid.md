# HMTI v2 — The 16 Housemate Types

## Axes

| # | Axis | Weight | Pole | Pole |
|---|------|--------|------|------|
| 1 | Cleanliness Standard | 35 | **N** Neat | **C** Casual |
| 2 | Social Intensity | 30 | **P** Private | **O** Open |
| 3 | Conflict Style | 20 | **D** Direct | **H** Harmonious |
| 4 | Structure Preference | 15 | **S** Structured | **F** Flexible |

- **Neat / Casual** — covers both hygiene (dishes, bathroom, trash) and clutter (visual tidiness); measure each with 2 questions and average.
- **Private / Open** — home as retreat vs. home as social space; includes sharing style (labeled shelves vs. communal groceries).
- **Direct / Harmonious** — when something bothers you, do you say it promptly, or keep the peace and absorb it? Measure with behavioral scenarios.
- **Structured / Flexible** — chore charts and guest policies upfront vs. figure it out as you go. Measure with preference statements.

Codes are collision-free with v1: v1 had S/F in position 3 and D/L in position 4; v2 has D/H in position 3 and S/F in position 4, so no v2 code ever existed in v1 — cached avatars and copy can't be confused across versions.

"Connects best with" = the three non-identical types scoring ≥70: flip structure (85), flip conflict (80), flip social (70).

## The 16 Types

### NPDS — The Curator 🦉 (owl)
*Neat · Private · Direct · Structured*
Runs a calm, beautiful, orderly home and tells you clearly how it works. Spotless shared spaces, respected closed doors, zero passive aggression — issues get raised once, kindly, and solved.
**Connects best with:** The Strategist (NPDF), The Minimalist (NPHS), The Captain (NODS)

### NPDF — The Strategist 🦊 (fox)
*Neat · Private · Direct · Flexible*
Clean, self-contained, and capable. No chore chart needed — they just handle things, and if something's off they'll say so plainly and move on. Independent without being distant.
**Connects best with:** The Curator (NPDS), The Clean Ghost (NPHF), The Firecracker (NODF)

### NPHS — The Minimalist 🐱 (cat)
*Neat · Private · Harmonious · Structured*
Quiet, elegant, low-drama. Loves a system precisely because good systems mean nobody ever has to have an awkward conversation. The roommate you forget is home — in the best way.
**Connects best with:** The Clean Ghost (NPHF), The Curator (NPDS), The Sweetheart (NOHS)

### NPHF — The Clean Ghost ✨ (ferret)
*Neat · Private · Harmonious · Flexible*
Rarely seen, never a problem. Keeps shared spaces spotless on their own schedule and creates zero friction. The most frictionless roommate in the system.
**Connects best with:** The Minimalist (NPHS), The Strategist (NPDF), The Sunshine (NOHF)

### NODS — The Captain 🐕 (golden retriever)
*Neat · Open · Direct · Structured*
Friendly leader who runs the house with warmth and systems — snack runs, chore charts, group chats. Says what needs saying and makes everyone feel at home doing it.
**Connects best with:** The Firecracker (NODF), The Sweetheart (NOHS), The Curator (NPDS)

### NODF — The Firecracker 🦜 (parrot)
*Neat · Open · Direct · Flexible*
Spontaneous plans, full living room, surprisingly clean counters. Big social energy with no filter problem — if something's off, you'll hear about it warmly and directly.
**Connects best with:** The Captain (NODS), The Sunshine (NOHF), The Strategist (NPDF)

### NOHS — The Sweetheart 🐶 (corgi)
*Neat · Open · Harmonious · Structured*
Warm, tidy, dependable — the universally liked roommate. Keeps routines, keeps the peace, keeps the kitchen clean. The home feels nicer because they're in it.
**Connects best with:** The Sunshine (NOHF), The Captain (NODS), The Minimalist (NPHS)

### NOHF — The Sunshine 🐼 (red panda)
*Neat · Open · Harmonious · Flexible*
Easygoing warmth with clean habits. Up for whatever, allergic to drama, somehow the apartment is always tidy and the mood is always good.
**Connects best with:** The Sweetheart (NOHS), The Firecracker (NODF), The Clean Ghost (NPHF)

### CPDS — The Anchor 🐢 (turtle)
*Casual · Private · Direct · Structured*
Steady, grounded, unflappable. Relaxed about mess but crystal clear about space and expectations — you always know exactly where you stand with them.
**Connects best with:** The Maverick (CPDF), The Unbothered (CPHS), The Glue (CODS)

### CPDF — The Maverick 🦔 (hedgehog)
*Casual · Private · Direct · Flexible*
Independent, low-maintenance, refreshingly blunt. Lives on their own schedule, doesn't sweat the small stuff, and tells you straight when something actually matters.
**Connects best with:** The Anchor (CPDS), The Cryptid (CPHF), The Ringleader (CODF)

### CPHS — The Unbothered 🐼 (panda)
*Casual · Private · Harmonious · Structured*
Peak chill with a steady rhythm. No drama, no demands, no surprises — just a calm presence who respects your space and expects the same.
**Connects best with:** The Cryptid (CPHF), The Anchor (CPDS), The Teddy Bear (COHS)

### CPHF — The Cryptid 🦇 (bat)
*Casual · Private · Harmonious · Flexible*
Sighted occasionally near the fridge at 2am. Pays rent on time, bothers no one, asks nothing. Legend says they live here.
**Connects best with:** The Unbothered (CPHS), The Maverick (CPDF), The Disco Ball (COHF)

### CODS — The Glue 🫶 (capybara)
*Casual · Open · Direct · Structured*
Holds the household together. Communal dinners, fair splits, real talk when it's needed — relaxed about mess, serious about everyone feeling included.
**Connects best with:** The Ringleader (CODF), The Teddy Bear (COHS), The Anchor (CPDS)

### CODF — The Ringleader 🦝 (raccoon)
*Casual · Open · Direct · Flexible*
The instigator of the good kind of chaos. Spontaneous hangs, open-door energy, and zero hesitation about naming a problem — then turning it into a house meeting that's actually fun.
**Connects best with:** The Glue (CODS), The Disco Ball (COHF), The Maverick (CPDF)

### COHS — The Teddy Bear 🐻 (bear)
*Casual · Open · Harmonious · Structured*
Cozy, warm, reliable. Makes the apartment feel like home — tea on the stove, blanket on the couch, steady routines, and never a harsh word.
**Connects best with:** The Disco Ball (COHF), The Glue (CODS), The Unbothered (CPHS)

### COHF — The Disco Ball 🦦 (otter)
*Casual · Open · Harmonious · Flexible*
The living room is a lifestyle. Spontaneous, sunny, up for anything, mad at no one. Brings the energy and rolls with absolutely everything.
**Connects best with:** The Teddy Bear (COHS), The Ringleader (CODF), The Cryptid (CPHF)

## Compatibility scoring

Same letter on an axis earns that axis's weight; max 100.

| Score | Band |
|-------|------|
| 85–100 | Excellent Match |
| 70–84 | Strong Match |
| 55–69 | Moderate Match |
| 40–54 | Risky Match |
| 0–39 | Challenging Match |

Note for v2 implementation: incorporate the `strength` field (55–95) rather than binary pole matching — a mild-Neat and mild-Casual pair should score partial credit, not zero.

## Practical constraints (outside HMTI)
Budget, location, move-in date, lease duration, pets, smoking/vaping, WFH status, guest frequency, sleep schedule, strict food boundaries, dealbreakers.
